About me

Design, code & data — under one roof.

I’m Benjamin Nana Kofi, a designer and developer who loves turning ideas into products that look great, work smoothly and get better with every release.

Most projects stall at the hand-off between design, engineering and analytics. I work across all three, so I can take something from a rough sketch to a shipped product — and then use real data to decide what to improve next.

When I’m not building, I’m exploring new data-viz techniques and learning from the design community.
